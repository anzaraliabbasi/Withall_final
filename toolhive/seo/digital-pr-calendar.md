# Digital PR Calendar — quarterly plan

Goal: earn real links. Rule: pitch stories, not products. Nobody links to a tool; they link to data and usefulness.

## Standing monthly slots
| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Publish micro-study from on-site behavior (e.g. "most-completed PDF tasks, 30-day sample") on the blog/About, pitch to 5 dev/design newsletters |
| 2 | Reddit/community week: 3 genuinely helpful comments using prompt #5 (r/webdev, r/productivity, r/pdf) — no links unless asked |
| 3 | Free-backlinks checklist pass (see free-backlinks.md): directories, profiles |
| 4 | One guest angle: offer a data post or expert quote to one publication; follow up previous pitches |

## Quarterly anchors (repeat yearly)
- **Q1** "New year productivity" — angle: privacy-first tooling (nothing uploads).
- **Q2** Developer-month angles: launch changelog posts, open-source snippets.
- **Q3** Back-to-school / student angle: free alternatives to paid software lists.
- **Q4** Ecommerce holiday prep: social-media mockup tools, image compression before holidays.

## Tracking
- Log every pitch (date, outlet, contact, status) in [sheet link].
- Measure: new referring domains / month (target: 3–5 after month 3), broken-link repairs, brand searches.
