# 8 Product Prompts (AI workflows for ToolHive content & growth)

Ready-to-use prompts for any AI assistant (including your Superagent). Copy, fill the [tool], run.

## 1. Tool SEO page polish
> You are an SEO copywriter for ToolHive, a free 100% client-side online toolbox. Rewrite the
> "What is" and FAQ blocks for the [tool] page. Answer the query "[primary keyword]" in the first
> 60 words, use plain language, add concrete limits, never fluff. Output: intro (50–70 words),
> 4–6 FAQ Q&As (30–50 words each).

## 2. FAQ mining (People Also Ask)
> List the 10 questions real users ask about [topic, e.g. compressing PDFs], ordered by search
> intent frequency. Mark which 5 belong on the [tool] FAQ block. Answers must be accurate for a
> browser-based tool (no uploads, no server).

## 3. Category hub refresh
> Here is our [category] page copy: [paste]. Rewrite to 150 words max: what the category does,
> why local processing matters, and weave in these keywords: [list]. Keep the same structure.

## 4. Internal linking map
> Given this list of tool URLs [paste], for the target page [URL] pick the 5 most related tools
> and write a natural anchor-phrase for each link. Avoid generic anchors like "click here".

## 5. Reddit/community answer
> Write a helpful, non-salesy Reddit comment answering: "[question, e.g. how do I convert PNG to
> JPG without uploading my files anywhere?]". Rules: 120 words max, lead with the actual solution,
> mention our tool only if genuinely relevant, disclose it's ours. No marketing language.

## 6. Monthly report digest
> Here is the GSC export for [month] (paste CSV summary): [data]. Write a 1-page monthly SEO
> report using our template sections (Traffic / Trust / AI answers / Sales / Priorities). Be
> specific with numbers, no vague conclusions.

## 7. Competitor gap scan
> Compare our [tool] page with these competitors: [paste 2–3 URLs' H2s]. Identify content gaps,
> missing FAQ questions, and one improvement that does not exist on any competitor.

## 8. PR pitch email
> Draft a 90-word pitch email to [publication/blog] pitching a data-led story: "[angle, e.g.
> we analyzed which online tools people actually finish using]". Mention ToolHive's data
> naturally in the last line, not the first. Subject line ≤ 45 characters.
