## Writing rules
- Answer the primary query in the first 60 words.
- No fluff intro, no "in today's digital world".
- Use the keyword naturally in H1, first paragraph, one H2, image alt if applicable.
- Include concrete numbers/limits ("up to 100 MB", "files never leave your device") — these earn trust snippets.
